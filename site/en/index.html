What is in the app

Everything below is in the current version.

Six languages

Arabic, German, English, Spanish, French and Turkish. Choose your own language and the one you learn.

Ten lessons per language

A1 lessons with everyday words and phrases, and short exercises after each. You pass at 70% correct answers.

Smart review

Words you learned come back after a day, then three days, then a week and so on, depending on how well you remember them.

Daily streak

Follow your streak of study days and your daily goal in minutes to keep the habit.

Fully Arabic

A right-to-left Arabic interface in a clear typeface, with an English version.

Light and dark

Light and dark themes with checked contrast so text stays readable.

Works offline

All lessons and exercises are on your device, so you can learn anywhere.

Private, no ads

No ads and no tracking, and your data never leaves your device.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. Choose your languages

    Your own language, then the one you want to learn.

  2. Start a lesson

    Words and phrases with meanings in your language, then short exercises.

  3. Review your words

    Words return at the right time, and you follow your progress and streak.

Project status

This is an early version: A1 only for now, sign-in is a demo and your data is stored only on your device. More levels and features are planned, without a promised date.
