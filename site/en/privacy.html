Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Lughati's lessons and exercises work on your device without a connection. The account, progress sync across your devices and the AI tutor need the internet.

What we collect

  • Account data: the email address and name you enter, and an internal account id. Your password goes straight to the authentication service over an encrypted connection; we never see it or store it in clear text.
  • Learning data: your own language and the language you learn, completed lessons and quiz results, word-review schedule, activity days and streak, and your settings (theme, interface language).
  • AI tutor usage: the request type, tokens used and date, to enforce usage limits and control cost. We do not store the text of your messages on our servers.

AI tutor

When you use it, your message and a few earlier turns of the conversation, together with your language and level, are sent to our server and from there to an AI model provider to produce the reply. We do not send your name, email or account id to the provider. Do not type sensitive personal information into messages. Answers can contain mistakes.

Where data is stored

On your device and in a cloud database (Supabase) protected by rules that stop any user from reaching another user's data. Session tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.

What does not happen

No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Word pronunciation

The listen button reads words with your device's text-to-speech engine; only the word text is passed to it. Some engines may use the internet for certain voices under their provider's policy.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete account" permanently deletes your account and all your cloud data. Signing out removes the session from the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the local copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
