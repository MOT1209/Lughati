Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Lughati currently works fully offline.

What is stored, and where

Only on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • The name and email you enter when creating an account, and the sign-in time. Your password is never stored.
  • Your own language, the language you learn, your lesson progress and word-review schedule.
  • Your settings: theme and interface language.

What does not happen

The app sends no data to any server, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties.

Word pronunciation

The listen button reads lesson words with your device's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google's speech engine on Android). The app itself sends nothing; only the lesson word is passed to the engine, never your name, email or progress. Some engines may use the internet for certain voices; that is governed by your device settings and the engine provider's policy.

Deleting data

Signing out removes the session. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in the device settings deletes all of the above permanently.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If online accounts or sync are added later, this policy will be updated first.
